---

### 💡 Key Technical Highlights & Features:

🔍 **1. Multimodal Wardrobe Vision Ingestion (Google Gemini)**
Users upload photos of their clothing, and the AI extracts structured sartorial metadata:
- Exact hex colors, color temperature, and palette classification
- Fabric textures (raw denim, ribbed knit, linen, wool flannel)
- Fit and silhouette (tailored, relaxed, oversized)
- Formality ratings (from casual lounging to black-tie formal)

🎨 **2. Algorithmic Color Harmony Engine**
Applies classical color physics and mathematical formulas in polar HSL coordinates:
- 60-30-10 dominant / secondary / accent rule
- Monochromatic, Analogous, and Complementary harmony scoring
- Neutral anchoring (navy, charcoal, olive, cream balance)

🧩 **3. Combinatorial Constraint Outfit Solver**
Solves multi-piece outfit combinations (Top + Bottom + Footwear + Outerwear) dynamically tailored to the user's selected occasion, temperature, and live weather.

🧠 **4. Explainable AI (XAI) Stylist Rationale**
Instead of black-box suggestions, the AI delivers an editorial stylist critique breaking down *why* an outfit works:
- Curatorial vision & texture dialogue
- Color harmony physics
- Silhouette proportion balance
- Pro styling tips (French tuck, cuff rolls, trouser break)

🧍‍♂️ **5. Interactive 3D Mannequin Fitting Room (Three.js)**
Real-time 3D visualization allowing users to preview and inspect their styled combinations before stepping out.

### Description:
Built a full-stack AI wardrobe intelligence platform designed to eliminate daily outfit fatigue and promote sustainable fashion by maximizing wear from existing clothing.

**Key Achievements & Architectures:**
- Developed a computer vision ingestion pipeline using Google Gemini to extract 14 structured attributes (fabric texture, hex color, silhouette, formality score) from user garment photos.
- Implemented an algorithmic color harmony engine utilizing polar HSL coordinates to compute chromatic distances and apply color theory rules (60-30-10 rule, complementary contrast, neutral anchoring).
- Engineered a combinatorial constraint solver generating multi-garment outfit pairings optimized for target occasions, formality scores, and weather conditions.
- Integrated an Explainable AI (XAI) stylist engine providing transparent editorial rationale and micro-styling recommendations for every suggested outfit.
- Built an interactive 3D mannequin fitting room using Three.js for real-time outfit preview.
- Deployed a scalable production architecture utilizing React 19, Vite, TypeScript, Express, Supabase PostgreSQL, and Render/Vercel CI/CD.
